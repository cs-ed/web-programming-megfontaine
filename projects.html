M.Ed. Capstone Project

I recently completed my Master of Education in Education Technology and Instructional Design.
This is the capstone research project I completed in the final phase of my program. In this project I explored
the impacts of e-learning on self-perception of educators teaching computer science without having prior training
in the field. The topic was inspired by my own experiences as a credentialed math teacher who taught myself
Java, Python, and block-based programming to offer these courses to students at my school.
